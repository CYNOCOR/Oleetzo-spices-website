Our Story

A shorter, fresher path between the spice gardens of Sri Lanka and the kitchens that use them.

WHERE IT STARTED

Spice, the way it used to taste.

Sri Lanka has shaped the world's spice trade for over a thousand years — its cinnamon alone once drew traders across oceans. Somewhere between the garden and the supermarket shelf, though, a lot of that character gets lost: spices sit in warehouses, get blended for volume rather than flavour, and arrive tasting like very little at all.

Oleetzo exists to shorten that path. We work directly with a small number of family gardens across the hill country, pay fairly for what they grow, and move each harvest to a jar quickly enough that the aroma is still there when it arrives.

WHAT WE HOLD TO

Four things we won't compromise on.

I.

Provenance

Every product is traceable to a named region and, in most cases, a specific garden.

II.

Purity

No fillers, no anti-caking agents, no irradiation. Just the spice, dried and milled.

III.

Fair Partnership

Growers are paid a fixed, transparent rate — agreed before the season, not after.

IV.

Craftsmanship

Traditional sun-drying and stone-milling, done at a scale small enough to do properly.
