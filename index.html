OUR STORY

Rooted in Sri Lanka's spice gardens.

Oleetzo began as a simple frustration: spice tins that tasted of nothing by the time they reached the shelf. We went back to the source — the same hill-country gardens that have supplied the island's spice trade for generations — and built a shorter, fresher path from harvest to home.

  • Direct relationships with the families who grow and harvest each spice
  • Sun-drying and stone-milling done the traditional way, at a small scale
  • Fair, transparent pricing paid straight to the growers

HOW IT'S MADE

From garden to jar.

HARVEST

Picked at peak ripeness by hand, garden by garden.

SUN-DRY

Cured slowly in the open air to hold on to natural oils.

HAND-SORT

Every batch is picked over for size, colour and quality.

SMALL-BATCH MILL

Stone-ground or left whole, in quantities we can sell fast.

SEAL FRESH

Packed airtight the same week, never left to sit in a warehouse.
